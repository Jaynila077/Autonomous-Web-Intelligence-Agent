The current job market scenario for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is highly competitive. With the increasing demand for AI and machine learning technologies, companies are looking for skilled professionals who can develop and implement these technologies. However, the job market is also highly saturated, with many qualified candidates vying for a limited number of positions. To stand out in this market, it is essential for fresher AI/ML engineers to have a strong foundation in programming languages such as Python, Java, and C++, as well as experience with popular AI and machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-learn. Additionally, having a good understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design patterns is crucial. Furthermore, it is important for fresher AI/ML engineers to stay up-to-date with the latest developments in the field by attending conferences, meetups, and webinars, and by participating in online communities and forums. Overall, the job market scenario for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is challenging, but with the right skills, knowledge, and attitude, it is possible to succeed and build a successful career in this field.
