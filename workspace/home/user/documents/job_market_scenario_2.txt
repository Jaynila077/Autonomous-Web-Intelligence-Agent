The current job market scenario for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is highly competitive. With the increasing demand for AI and machine learning technologies, companies are looking for skilled professionals who can develop and implement these technologies. However, the supply of skilled AI/ML engineers is limited, leading to a competitive job market. To increase their chances of getting hired, fresher AI/ML engineers should focus on developing their skills in programming languages such as Python, R, and Julia, as well as in machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch. They should also have a strong understanding of computer vision, natural language processing, and deep learning. Additionally, having a portfolio of projects that demonstrate their skills and experience can be beneficial in getting hired. Overall, the job market scenario for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is challenging, but with the right skills and experience, it is possible to succeed.
