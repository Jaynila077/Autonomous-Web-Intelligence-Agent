# Future Trends in Agentic AI

## 1. Increased Autonomy
- **Description**: Future AI systems will have greater autonomy, enabling them to operate independently in more complex environments.

## 2. Enhanced Collaboration
- **Description**: AI systems will increasingly collaborate with humans and other AI systems, leading to more effective teamwork.

## 3. Ethical AI Development
- **Description**: There will be a stronger focus on ethical considerations in AI development, ensuring responsible use of technology.

## 4. Integration with IoT
- **Description**: Agentic AI will be integrated with Internet of Things (IoT) devices, leading to smarter and more responsive systems.
