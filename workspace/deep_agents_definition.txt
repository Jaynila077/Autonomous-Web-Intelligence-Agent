# Definition of Deep Agents

Deep agents are advanced artificial intelligence systems that utilize deep learning techniques to perform tasks autonomously. They are designed to learn from vast amounts of data, adapt to new situations, and make decisions based on their experiences. Deep agents can operate in complex environments and are often characterized by their ability to process high-dimensional data, such as images, audio, and text.

## Characteristics of Deep Agents:
1. **Autonomy**: Deep agents can operate independently without human intervention.
2. **Learning**: They utilize deep learning algorithms to improve their performance over time.
3. **Adaptability**: Deep agents can adjust their strategies based on changing environments or new information.
4. **Decision-Making**: They can analyze data and make informed decisions based on their learning.
5. **Multi-Modal Processing**: Deep agents can handle various types of data inputs, including visual, auditory, and textual information.
