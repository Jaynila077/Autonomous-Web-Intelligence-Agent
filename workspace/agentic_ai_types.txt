# Types of Agentic AI

Agentic AI can be categorized into several types based on their operational characteristics and decision-making processes. The main types include:

## 1. Reactive Agents
- **Definition**: Reactive agents operate based on current perceptions without internal models of the world. They respond to stimuli in real-time.
- **Characteristics**: Simple, fast, and effective in predictable environments. They do not learn from past experiences.
- **Examples**: Basic robotic vacuum cleaners that navigate using sensors to avoid obstacles.

## 2. Deliberative Agents
- **Definition**: Deliberative agents maintain internal models of the world and can plan their actions based on these models.
- **Characteristics**: Capable of reasoning, planning, and learning from experiences. They can adapt their strategies based on past outcomes.
- **Examples**: Chess-playing programs that evaluate multiple moves ahead and choose the best strategy.

## 3. Hybrid Agents
- **Definition**: Hybrid agents combine the features of both reactive and deliberative agents, allowing them to operate effectively in dynamic environments.
- **Characteristics**: They can react quickly to immediate stimuli while also planning for future actions based on learned experiences.
- **Examples**: Autonomous vehicles that navigate traffic while also planning routes based on real-time data.
