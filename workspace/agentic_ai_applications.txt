# Applications of Agentic AI

## 1. Robotics
- **Description**: Agentic AI is used in robots for navigation, manipulation, and interaction with humans.
- **Examples**: Autonomous drones, robotic vacuum cleaners, and industrial robots.

## 2. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- **Description**: AI architectures enable machines to understand and generate human language.
- **Examples**: Chatbots, virtual assistants, and translation services.

## 3. Autonomous Systems
- **Description**: Agentic AI powers systems that operate independently in complex environments.
- **Examples**: Self-driving cars, smart traffic management systems, and autonomous delivery vehicles.
