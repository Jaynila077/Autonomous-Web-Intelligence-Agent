# Ethical Considerations in Agentic AI

## 1. Accountability
- **Description**: Determining who is responsible for the actions of AI systems.
- **Importance**: Essential for trust and transparency in AI applications.

## 2. Bias and Fairness
- **Description**: Ensuring AI systems do not perpetuate or amplify biases present in training data.
- **Importance**: Critical for equitable outcomes in AI applications.

## 3. Privacy
- **Description**: Protecting user data and ensuring consent in data usage.
- **Importance**: Vital for maintaining user trust and complying with regulations.

## 4. Safety
- **Description**: Ensuring AI systems operate safely and do not cause harm.
- **Importance**: Essential for applications in critical areas like healthcare and transportation.
