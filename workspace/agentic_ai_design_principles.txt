# Design Principles of Agentic AI Architectures

## 1. Autonomy
- **Definition**: The ability of an AI system to operate independently without human intervention.
- **Importance**: Enables systems to make decisions and take actions based on their environment and goals.

## 2. Adaptability
- **Definition**: The capacity to learn from experiences and adjust to new situations.
- **Importance**: Essential for long-term effectiveness in dynamic environments.

## 3. Goal-Oriented Behavior
- **Definition**: The focus on achieving specific objectives.
- **Importance**: Ensures that AI systems prioritize actions that lead to desired outcomes.

## 4. Interaction and Collaboration
- **Definition**: The ability to communicate and work with other agents or humans.
- **Importance**: Enhances the effectiveness of AI systems in complex tasks requiring teamwork.

## 5. Ethical Considerations
- **Definition**: The integration of moral principles in AI decision-making.
- **Importance**: Ensures that AI systems operate within acceptable societal norms and values.
