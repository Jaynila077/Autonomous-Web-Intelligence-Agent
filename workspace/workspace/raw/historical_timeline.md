# Historical Timeline of Transformers

## Pre-Transformer Era (2014-2016)
- **2014**: Bahdanau et al. introduce attention mechanism for neural machine translation (seq2seq with attention).
- **2015**: Luong et al. propose global attention for better context handling in NMT.
- **2014-2016**: Sutskever et al. develop seq2seq models with encoder-decoder architecture.

## Foundational (2017-2018)
- **2017**: Vaswani et al. publish "Attention Is All You Need", introducing the Transformer architecture with self-attention and multi-head attention.
- **2018**: Devlin et al. introduce BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) with masked language modeling.
- **2018**: Radford et al. release GPT-1, a decoder-only Transformer for language modeling.

## Scaling Era (2019-2020)
- **2019**: Dai et al. introduce Transformer-XL with recurrence for longer context.
- **2019**: Beltagy et al. propose Longformer with sliding window attention for efficient long sequences.
- **2019**: Child et al. present GPT-2 with improved scaling and zero-shot capabilities.
- **2019**: Liu et al. optimize BERT training for RoBERTa.
- **2019**: Raffel et al. introduce T5, a text-to-text framework for various NLP tasks.
- **2020**: Kitaev et al. propose Reformer with locality-sensitive hashing for efficient attention.
- **2020**: Choromanski et al. introduce Performer with kernel-based linear attention.
- **2020**: Dao et al. develop FlashAttention for memory-efficient attention computation.
- **2020**: Fedus et al. introduce Switch Transformer with sparse Mixture-of-Experts.
- **2020**: Kaplan et al. establish scaling laws for neural language models.
- **2020**: Brown et al. release GPT-3 with 175B parameters and few-shot learning.

## Efficiency & Specialization (2021-2022)
- **2021**: Su et al. introduce RoFormer with rotary position embeddings.
- **2022**: Press et al. propose Train Short, Test Long with linear biases.
- **2022**: Touvron et al. release LLaMA, an open foundation model.
- **2022**: Ouyang et al. introduce InstructGPT with RLHF for instruction following.
- **2022**: Bai et al. present Constitutional AI for safe alignment.
- **2022**: Various efficiency improvements including FlashAttention 2, Mamba, and other SSM variants.

## Multimodal & Emerging (2022-Present)
- **2022**: Dosovitskiy et al. introduce Vision Transformer (ViT) for image classification.
- **2021**: Radford et al. release CLIP for contrastive image-text learning.
- **2022**: Chen et al. develop DALL-E for autoregressive image generation.
- **2023**: Li et al. introduce LLaVA for vision-language tasks.
- **2022**: Rombach et al. release Stable Diffusion for latent diffusion image synthesis.
- **2023**: Mamba (SSM-based) models for linear-time sequence modeling.
- **2024**: Ongoing research into efficient attention mechanisms, long-context scaling, and multimodal unification.
