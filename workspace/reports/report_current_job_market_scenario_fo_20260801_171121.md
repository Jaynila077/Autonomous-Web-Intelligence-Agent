## Executive Summary & Core Insights

The current job market scenario for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is highly competitive, with a growing demand for skilled professionals in this field. The average salary range for AI/ML engineers in fresher roles is between $100,000 to $150,000 per annum, depending on the location, company, and specific job requirements.
