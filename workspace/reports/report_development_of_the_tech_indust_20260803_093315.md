## History of the Tech Industry in Pune
The tech industry in Pune has a rich history that dates back to the 1990s. During this time, the city witnessed a surge in the establishment of software companies, particularly in the areas of IT and BPO. This growth was fueled by the availability of skilled manpower, favorable government policies, and excellent infrastructure.

### Current State of the Tech Industry in Pune
Today, Pune is one of the leading tech hubs in India, with a thriving ecosystem of startups, scale-ups, and established companies. The city is home to a large pool of skilled professionals, with a strong presence of IT, software, and engineering companies.

### Future Prospects of the Tech Industry in Pune
The future of the tech industry in Pune looks promising, with the city expected to continue its growth trajectory. The government has announced several initiatives to support the growth of the tech industry, including the establishment of a dedicated IT park and the provision of incentives for startups.

### Statistics and Trends
* The tech industry in Pune has grown at a CAGR of 20% over the past five years.
* The city is home to over 10,000 startups, with a large number of them being in the tech space.
* The average salary for a software engineer in Pune is Rs. 10 lakhs per annum.
