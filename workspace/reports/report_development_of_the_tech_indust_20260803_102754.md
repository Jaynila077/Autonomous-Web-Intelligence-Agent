## Executive Summary & Core Insights

The tech industry in Bangalore has undergone significant transformations since its inception. From its humble beginnings to the current status as a global tech hub, the city has witnessed tremendous growth and development.

### History

The tech industry in Bangalore began to take shape in the 1980s with the establishment of Texas Instruments, the first multinational corporation to set up operations in the city. This was followed by the setting up of other major players such as Infosys, Wipro, and HCL.

### Current State

Today, Bangalore is home to over 3,000 startups and 400,000 IT professionals, making it one of the largest tech hubs in the world. The city is also a major center for artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics.

### Future Prospects

The future of the tech industry in Bangalore looks promising, with the city expected to continue its growth trajectory. The government has also launched several initiatives to support the growth of the tech industry, including the setting up of a dedicated startup hub and the provision of funding for startups.
