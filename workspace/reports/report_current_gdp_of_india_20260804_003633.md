### Deep Technical System Architecture & Workflows

The GDP of India is calculated by the Central Statistics Office (CSO) using the expenditure approach. The CSO collects data on various components of GDP, including private consumption, government spending, investment, and net exports. The data is then compiled and analyzed to calculate the GDP.
