# Types of Agentic AI Architectures

## 1. Reactive Agents
- **Description**: These agents respond to stimuli from their environment without internal state or memory.
- **Strengths**: Simple and fast; suitable for well-defined tasks.
- **Weaknesses**: Lack of learning and adaptability.

## 2. Deliberative Agents
- **Description**: These agents maintain an internal model of the world and can plan actions based on that model.
- **Strengths**: Capable of complex decision-making and planning.
- **Weaknesses**: Computationally intensive and may struggle in dynamic environments.

## 3. Hybrid Agents
- **Description**: Combine reactive and deliberative approaches to leverage the strengths of both.
- **Strengths**: Flexible and adaptable; can handle a variety of tasks.
- **Weaknesses**: Complexity in design and implementation.

## 4. Learning Agents
- **Description**: These agents improve their performance over time through learning from experiences.
- **Strengths**: Highly adaptable and capable of handling unforeseen situations.
- **Weaknesses**: Requires significant data and time to learn effectively.
