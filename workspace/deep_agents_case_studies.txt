# Case Studies of Successful Implementations of Deep Agents

## 1. DeepMind's AlphaGo
- **Overview**: AlphaGo is a deep reinforcement learning agent developed by DeepMind that defeated the world champion Go player, Lee Sedol, in 2016.
- **Significance**: This achievement demonstrated the potential of deep agents in mastering complex games and strategic thinking.

## 2. Tesla's Autopilot
- **Overview**: Tesla's Autopilot system uses deep learning algorithms to enable semi-autonomous driving capabilities in its vehicles.
- **Significance**: The system continuously learns from real-world driving data, improving its performance and safety over time.

## 3. IBM Watson
- **Overview**: IBM Watson is a cognitive computing system that uses deep learning to analyze vast amounts of data and provide insights in various fields, including healthcare and finance.
- **Significance**: Watson's ability to process natural language and learn from interactions has made it a valuable tool for decision-making in complex environments.
